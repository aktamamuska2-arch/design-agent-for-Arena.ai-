## Pose keys (pose-to-pose)

Each beat ships 2–3 poses only: contact, breakdown, extreme. The extremes that carry the film:

1. Egg C-shape squash (max compression at 2.55 s) — anticipation.
2. Hatch overshoot: chick stretched 1.45× tall at 2.78 s.
3. Sit-down impact: chick squashed to 1.6× wide / 0.55× tall at 9.05 s.
4. Pounce: full stretch along an arc at 14.45 s.
5. Hang-time at 24.55 s: no ground, no wind, wings still — the stillest frame in the film.
6. Final silhouette at 28.4 s: wings spread, sun behind.

## Staging

- Chick never shares the frame's centre with another strong shape; the eye always lands on the chick.
- Background contrast drops (haze, blur) whenever the chick must read as silhouette.
- Camera: slow push in act 1–2, whip on the hatch and the sit-down, wide→push for act 3,
  travelling follow for act 4, pull back and hold for act 5.

## Sound plan (120 BPM, beat = 0.5 s)

- Act 1: low drone + music box breath; crack = snap; hatch = pop + shimmer.
- Act 2: pizzicato waddle (legato on every step), slide whistle on the slip, woodblock thud on the sit.
- Act 3: flutter harp for the butterfly, whoosh on the pounce, small sad cello beat.
- Act 4: rising strings + wind, big swoosh on the swoop.
- Act 5: hang-time = music drops out (silence is the joke), then full theme, sun chime, warm resolve.
